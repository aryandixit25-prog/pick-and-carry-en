PICK AND CARRY SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS

Do’s

✔ Keep the Seat Belt tied all the time during operation.
✔ Check the tyre pressure & brakes daily before use.
✔ Check both horn and lights before operation.
✔ Check SLI and other interlocks for proper functioning.
✔ Switch OFF ignition and remove key when not in operation.
✔ Always use tested slings, D shackles and sling belts in good condition.
✔ Carry out load test yearly by a competent person and record it.

Don’ts

✖ Never lift load more than SWL.
✖ Never start engine with clutch operated and gear engaged.
✖ Do not use Pick and Carry to shift load for long distances.
✖ Never operate under influence of alcohol.
✖ Perform vision test of operator every six months.
✖ Never use mobile phone while operating.
✖ Never use damaged slings or D shackles for lifting.
✖ Do not use Pick and Carry for man lifting.
